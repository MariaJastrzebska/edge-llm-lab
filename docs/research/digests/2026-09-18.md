  targeting anatomical regions to reduce hallucinations in medical VLMs. A

  latency/energy/edge-hardware angle, no grammar/schema constraint) —
  different meaning of "decoding" than G1's mask-cost mechanism.
- **VALOR** (arXiv 2512.16201) — RL-based visual-grounding fix for radiology
  report generation, cloud-scale.
- **Anatomy-VLM** (arXiv 2511.08402) — fine-grained anatomical-ROI VLM for
  diagnostic accuracy, no deployment-target discussion.
- **Taming VLMs for Medical Image Analysis** (arXiv 2506.18378) — a 34-page
  review of adaptation strategies (pretraining/fine-tuning/prompt-learning);
  no edge-deployment chapter found.
- Two cloud-API-based clinical-report tools: **Blood Report Analysis**
  (arXiv 2509.06033, benchmarks Qwen-VL-Max/Gemini 2.5 Pro/Llama 4 Maverick
  via API) and **Intelligent Healthcare Imaging Platform** (arXiv 2509.13590,
  built on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash) — both explicitly cloud-hosted
  general-purpose VLMs, not on-device.
- **Visually-Guided Controllable Medical Image Generation** (arXiv
  2603.10519) — a training-time diffusion-model image-*synthesis* paper
  (privacy-preserving synthetic data), wrong task entirely (generation, not
  report/interpretation) — filed only to avoid re-discovery.

**Lite VLA** (arXiv 2511.05642, "Efficient Vision-Language-Action Control on
CPU-Bound Edge Robots") — **differentiate-from/cite**. A genuinely relevant
edge×robotics find: a compact VLM deployed on-board a mobile robot for
simultaneous movement and scene reasoning, GPS-denied/no-cloud-connectivity
framing, CPU-bound embedded hardware. No grammar-constrained/structured-
decoding angle and it's an action model (VLA) rather than structured
text/JSON generation, so it doesn't compete with G1's mechanism — but it's
the most concrete "robot that sees, on-device, real hardware" precedent
found yet for the paper's edge×robotics motivation, worth a related-work
sentence distinct from the split-computing cluster (CoVSpec/INAR-VL/LAST,
iter 36) since Lite VLA's target model never leaves the device.

**"Sometimes Painful but Certainly Promising"** (arXiv 2503.09114, ACM
TECS) — **cite**. A general (text, not VLM) on-device SLM feasibility/
trade-off characterization across quantization schemes, measuring memory,
latency, throughput, and energy on edge hardware — same methodological
shape as the already-filed 2511.11624 and "Sustainable LLM Inference for
Edge AI" (iter 27), another citable precedent for the harness-OS paper's
own telemetry/characterization methodology, no mechanism overlap.

Filed one 2023 paper, "Mobile Foundation Model as Firmware" (arXiv
