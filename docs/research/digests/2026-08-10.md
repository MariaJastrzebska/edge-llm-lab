# Research digest — 2026-08-10 (digest #1)

First sweep on brief questions 1–2. Headline: **the generic "quantized LLM
tool-calling on edge" space is standardizing fast — MLPerf now owns the generic
benchmark.** Good news: our defensible angles remain open (see verdicts).

## Q1 — Tool-calling of small quantized LLMs on edge

- **MLPerf Inference v6.1: Edge Agentic Inference benchmark** (MLCommons, Jul 2026)
  https://mlcommons.org/2026/07/mlperf-inference-v61-edge-agentic/
  Industry-standard benchmark: tool-calling accuracy (BFCL v4) + speed under an
  edge budget, Q4_K_M GGUF on a single edge accelerator.
  **Verdict: build-on + differentiate.** Use their setup as the calibration
  baseline our plan wanted; our paper must NOT be "another generic benchmark" —
  our room is: multi-turn conversational agents (they replay single agentic
  traces), energy accounting, runtime-flag ablations, task-domain suites
  (robot/medical).

- **SimpleTool: Parallel Decoding for Real-Time LLM Function Calling**
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.00030
  3–6× function-call speedup on Qwen 0.5B–14B across five FC benchmarks;
  sub-100ms calls with 4B models when combined with quantization.
  **Verdict: cite + candidate technique.** A decoding-level optimization our
  runtime-flag ablation grid could include as a condition.

- **A Systematic Evaluation of On-Device LLMs: Quantization, Performance, and
  Resources** (arXiv 2505.15030) https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15030
  0.5B–14B × seven PTQ methods on commodity hardware; key finding: heavily
  quantized LARGE models beat small high-precision ones; ~3.5 effective
  bits-per-weight threshold.
  **Verdict: MUST-CITE, closest prior work to the thesis result.** Our delta:
  they measure generic performance; we measure STRUCTURED TOOL-CALL FIDELITY in
  multi-turn agents + energy + runtime flags. Position explicitly against this.

## Q2 — Energy/latency/memory accounting on edge

- **ELIB: edge LLM inference benchmarking framework + MBU metric**
  (arXiv 2508.11269) https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11269
  Cross-platform edge inference benchmarking; proposes Memory-Bandwidth
  Utilization (MBU) metric.
  **Verdict: cite; adopt MBU** into our telemetry schema — cheap to compute,
  makes reviewers happy.

- **RooflineBench: On-Device LLM benchmarking via roofline analysis**
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11506
  **Verdict: cite** in measurement-methodology related work.

- **Cloud to Edge: Benchmarking LLM Inference on Hardware-Accelerated SBCs**
  (arXiv 2604.24785) https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24785v1
  Single-board computers (Pi-class) inference benchmarking.
  **Verdict: build-on** — supports the "buy a Pi 5" decision; their setup notes
  will save us bring-up time.

## Implications for the idea ranking (docs/research/2026-08-10-research-ideas.md)

1. Idea 1 sharpens to: **multi-turn agentic tool-calling fidelity × runtime
   optimization × energy on real edge devices, calibrated against MLPerf/BFCL** —
   the multi-turn + energy + domain-suite combination is the gap; the generic
   single-call benchmark is TAKEN (MLPerf).
2. The ~3.5 BPW threshold (2505.15030) is a testable hypothesis for OUR setting:
   does it hold for multi-turn structured tool-calling? A crisp, citable RQ.
3. Idea 3 (EdgeAgentBench) shrinks: the framework niche is crowding (ELIB,
   RooflineBench, MLPerf). Reposition as "domain task suites + multi-turn
   protocol on top of MLPerf", or fold into Idea 1's artifact release.
