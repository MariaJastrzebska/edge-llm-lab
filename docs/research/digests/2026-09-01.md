## Literature sweep — algorithmic mask-speedup cluster (per iter 24's queued option (a), 4 iterations stale)

Two genuinely new-to-`seen.json` items found:

**1. Trie Automata for Constrained Decoding over Large Finite Sets** (arXiv
2608.12574, Amazon — Xingzi Xu & Karim Bouyarmane, submitted 2026-08-12) —
**differentiate-from.** Exploits finite-set structure (shared prefixes,
bounded depth, known cardinality) via Aho-Corasick multi-pattern matching to
precompute per-node token masks, targeting the "cardinality wall" where
general grammar compilation gets slow once the number of valid values grows
into the thousands. Reports 7× faster per-step valid-token computation
(0.65µs vs. 5.8µs) than XGrammar and 2–6.5× faster compilation at K≥300.
Benchmarked explicitly against vLLM/SGLang backends — same defining trait as
every other cluster member: a genuine mask-computation speedup with no edge
hardware, no Joules, no single-stream framing. Joins the **algorithmic
mask-speedup cluster** (PSC, GRID, Attention-Meets-Reachability,
Token-Space-Compression, Pre³, XGrammar-2, MetaJuLS, Stay Within Your
Bounds) as its **8th member**. Worth flagging as the strongest raw-speedup
number in the cluster so far (7×) — still not competitive with G1/Route A
(finite-set/large-K masking is a different problem shape than JSON-schema
per-token masking on a short single-stream decode), but a citation to
position against carefully in any related-work section claiming "existing
mask-speedup work targets cloud batch serving."

**2. Lookahead-then-Verify: Reliable Constrained Decoding for Diffusion LLMs
under Context-Free Grammars (LAVE)** (arXiv 2602.00612, Feb 2026, Tsinghua/
PKU/Beihang/NUS) — **ignore.** Targets diffusion LLMs (dLLMs), a
fundamentally different generation paradigm (parallel token distributions
sampled and verified for grammar-extendability, not autoregressive
next-token decoding) — same reason iter 20's Diffusion-LLM-on-NPU item was
scored ignore. No relevance to G1/Route A's llama.cpp autoregressive
decode-time mask-cost claim; filed only so a future dLLM-curious sweep
