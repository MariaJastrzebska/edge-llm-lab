1. **BaseRT: Best-in-Class LLM Inference on Apple Silicon via Native Metal**
   (arXiv 2607.00501) — a native-Metal inference runtime beating llama.cpp
   decode throughput by 1.15–1.56× and MLX by up to 1.35× on M3/M4 Pro,
   tested on the Qwen3, Llama 3.2, and Gemma families — the *same* model
   families G1's five measurement rounds used. **Relevance:** high as
   context, not as competition. It doesn't touch grammar-constrained
   decoding at all, so it isn't a scoop. But it's evidence that (a) Apple
   Silicon decode throughput is an active, well-funded optimization target
   — useful ammunition for the "Apple isn't a natural environment" reviewer
   objection Maria raised (decision #8 in STATUS.md) — and (b) if a
   BaseRT-class runtime ever lands under llama.cpp/Ollama, G1's decode-speed
   baseline shifts and could move models across the crossover threshold.
   **Verdict: differentiate-from** (cite as engine-layer prior work distinct
   from grammar-mask cost; flag as a baseline-drift risk to watch, not an
   action item now).
2. **BaseRT: Advancing Best-in-Class LLM Inference with Apple M5 Neural
   Accelerators** (arXiv 2607.19438) — follow-up to #1, targeting the newer
   M5 chip's neural accelerators. Same verdict and reasoning as #1, one
   generation newer hardware. **Verdict: differentiate-from**, same caveat.
3. **CWCD: Category-Wise Contrastive Decoding for Structured Medical Report
   Generation** (arXiv 2604.10410, U. Buffalo) — generates radiology
   findings region-by-region with contrastive decoding to suppress
   hallucinated pathology co-occurrences. Same *application* space as Route
   A (structured medical report generation from an LM) but a completely
   different *mechanism* (contrastive decoding for accuracy/hallucination,
   not grammar masking for schema validity) and no edge/latency/energy
   angle at all — cloud-scale radiology foundation models (LLaVA-Rad,
   Maira-2) as its baselines. **Verdict: differentiate-from** — worth one
   related-work sentence distinguishing "we constrain the output schema
   at the token level for validity + cost" from "they suppress hallucinated
   content for accuracy," if Route A proceeds.
4. **A Comparative Study of MLX, MLC-LLM, Ollama, llama.cpp, ...** (arXiv
   2511.05502) — cross-engine throughput comparison. Backend-choice
   context, not a competitor. **Verdict: ignore** for the digest (already
   the kind of general engine-comparison ground both G1 and BaseRT sit on;
   nothing new about grammar/constrained decoding).
5. **ConsumerBench: Benchmarking Generative AI Applications on End-User
   Devices** (arXiv 2506.17538) — general on-device GenAI app benchmarking
   methodology, not decoding-tax specific. **Verdict: ignore.**
6. **LLM-Inference-Bench** (arXiv 2411.00136) — AI-accelerator inference
   benchmarking, generic, no grammar/schema angle. **Verdict: ignore.**
