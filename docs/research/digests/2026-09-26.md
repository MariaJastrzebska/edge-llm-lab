## New-to-seen.json items (7)

1. **"Embodied Foundation Models at the Edge: A Survey of Deployment
   Constraints and Mitigation Strategies"** (arXiv 2603.16952, Grover et al.,
   Mar 2026) — **differentiate-from/cite.** Proposes the "Deployment
   Gauntlet": eight coupled systems barriers (memory traffic, compute
   latency, timing variability, safety margins) that determine whether
   embodied foundation models run reliably at the edge. Finds autoregressive
   VLA policies are memory-bandwidth-bound while diffusion-based controllers
   are compute-latency-bound, and argues deployment is fundamentally a
   *systems co-design* problem, not a model-compression problem. Headline-ish
   related-work anchor: this framing — "systems problem, not compression" —
   is almost verbatim the harness-OS thesis's own motivating claim, just
   argued for the VLA/action-model family rather than LLM/VLM structured-text
   decoding. No grammar/mask-cost angle (there's no structured text output in
   this family to mask).

2. **"Embodied.cpp: A Portable Inference Runtime of Embodied AI Models on
   Heterogeneous Robots"** (arXiv 2607.02501, Jul 2026) — **differentiate-
   from/cite, headline find.** A llama.cpp-shaped portable C++ inference
   runtime purpose-built for VLA/world-action-model deployment across
   heterogeneous robot hardware: five-layer architecture (input adapters,
   sequence builders, backbone execution, head plugins, deployment adapters),
   multi-rate execution inside closed-loop control, latency-first batch-1
   inference, 1.05×–2.70× speedup and 7–77% lower VRAM vs. Python baselines.
   This is the closest "harness/runtime-OS for embodied edge AI" analog found
   in 48 iterations of sweeping — structurally parallel to bigos's own
   ambition, but for the action-model family instead of language models. Not
   a scoop (different model family, no constrained-decoding/grammar-mask
   angle since there is no structured text to mask) but worth a related-work
   sentence distinguishing runtime-layer scope (LLM/VLM decode vs. embodied
   action-model execution) if the whole-harness paper scope is confirmed.

3. **"Jetson-PI: Towards Onboard Real-Time Robot Control via
   Foresight-Aligned Asynchronous Inference"** (arXiv 2607.12659, Yang et
   al., Jul 2026) — **differentiate-from/cite.** Real Jetson Orin onboard VLA
   deployment; uses asynchronous inference to hide latency, then fixes the
   resulting perception-execution misalignment with a lightweight
   future-correction module. Fully on-device (no edge-cloud split), real
   robot hardware, real-time-control-loop-deadline framing — directly
   relevant to BRIEF.md's Stage-2 theme of "real-time guarantees for robot
   control loops." VLA action model, not structured JSON/text generation, no
   grammar/mask angle — not a mechanism competitor.

4. **"Medical Context Distorts Decisions in Clinical Vision Language
   Models"** (arXiv 2605.17436) — **ignore.** MIMIC-CXR chest-X-ray study of
   three VLM failure modes (text-over-image over-reliance, spurious reliance
   on irrelevant clinical history, prompt sensitivity). Continues the
   standing pattern (iters 10/17/21/24/26/28/32/36/40/44) that
   accuracy/robustness/bias-focused medical-VLM literature has zero
   systems-cost competitors on file; general-domain/cloud-scale VLMs, no edge
   deployment or latency/energy angle. Filed to avoid re-discovery.

5. **"EcoVLA: Energy-Efficient Device-Edge Co-Inference for
   Vision-Language-Action Models under Real-Time Constraints"** (arXiv
   2608.15502, Aug 2026) — **ignore for Route A/on-device framing.**
   Adaptive device-edge co-inference scheduler for VLA models, up to 236%
   system energy-efficiency improvement over fixed co-inference baselines
   under a 20Hz SLO. Same DynaSplit-shaped pattern as CoVSpec/INAR-VL/LAST —
   the workload leaves the device by design, which weakens rather than
   serves an on-device-privacy framing. VLA not structured-text generation,
   no grammar/mask angle. Filed to avoid re-discovery.

6. **"RAPID: Redundancy-Aware and Compatibility-Optimal Edge-Cloud
   Partitioned Inference for Diverse VLA Models"** (arXiv 2603.07949, Zheng/
   Tian et al., Mar 2026) — **ignore.** Model-hardware co-aware edge-cloud
   split-point search for VLA models exploiting step-wise action redundancy,
   1.73× speedup at 5–7% overhead. Same DynaSplit-shaped edge-cloud-split
   pattern. Filed to avoid re-discovery.

7. **"RoboECC: Multi-Factor-Aware Edge-Cloud Collaborative Deployment for VLA
   Models"** (arXiv 2603.20711, IJCNN 2026) — **ignore.** Model-hardware
   co-aware segmentation + network-aware adjustment for VLA edge-cloud split
   inference, 3.16–3.28× speedup on Orin+A100, 2.10–2.23× on Thor+A100 over
   edge-only. Same DynaSplit-shaped cluster, now six members on file
   (DynaSplit, CoVSpec, INAR-VL, LAST, EcoVLA, RAPID, RoboECC — all weaken
   rather than serve on-device privacy by splitting inference across the
   network). Filed to avoid re-discovery.
