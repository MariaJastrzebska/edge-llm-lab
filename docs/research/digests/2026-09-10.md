## Headline finding: AgentStop — the first hit combining harness-level mechanism with real edge energy telemetry

**AgentStop: Terminating Local AI Agents Early to Save Energy in Consumer
Devices** (arXiv 2605.15206, accepted ACM CAIS '26; Brave Research) —
[https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15206)

*Relevance:* A lightweight "efficiency supervisor" sits inside an agent's
harness and preemptively terminates trajectories it predicts won't succeed,
using cheap signals (token-level log-probabilities). Measured on real
consumer-device hardware (GPU power draw, temperature, battery drain), it
cuts wasted energy 15–20% with <5% utility drop on web-QA/coding benchmarks.
*Verdict:* **differentiate-from, high priority.** This directly answers
iteration 31's open question — does anyone publish a paper that pairs
"SLM/agent harness" framing WITH real edge-hardware energy measurement in the
same paper? Yes. It is not a scoop of G1 (its mechanism is agent-loop
early-stopping, an orchestration-level policy — not a decode-time/kernel
mechanism like G1's mask-cost tax + shadow-mask pipelining), but it is the
strongest concrete precedent yet that "harness mechanism + edge telemetry"
papers get accepted at real venues. Sharpens rather than threatens bigos's
required contribution: the paper needs to be explicit that its mechanisms
live one layer down from AgentStop's (kernel/runtime/decode-time, not
agent-loop-control), or a reviewer will ask "how is this different from
AgentStop's framing."

## Other new items

**CLONE: Customizing LLMs for Efficient Latency-Aware Inference at the Edge**
(arXiv 2506.02847, USENIX ATC '25, U. Macau) —
[https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02847)
Joint offline device-specific model tailoring (pruning via an
encoder-evaluator-decoder search) + online latency-aware system
optimization; up to 11.92× inference speedup and 7.36× energy savings on
off-the-shelf edge platforms. **Differentiate-from** — no grammar/mask
angle, but the most complete "systems framework for edge LLM inference"
paper found to date; joins the general-purpose inference-engine speedup
cluster as a watch-not-act baseline-drift item (different mechanism class:
model tailoring, not constrained decoding).

**Agent Harness for Large Language Model Agents: A Survey** (arXiv
2605.29682) —
[https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29682)
Third harness-taxonomy survey found (after 2604.08224, 2604.18071): 110+
papers, 23 systems, formalizes the harness as six governable components
(Execution, Tools, Context, Scope, Loop, Verification — "ETCSLV") plus a
"Harness Completeness Matrix." **Cite/differentiate-from** — same
application/agent-orchestration level as the other two, no edge/SLM/
telemetry angle. Three independent harness-taxonomy surveys now on file;
the terminology precedent for "harness" as a research object is well
established, none touch edge hardware.

**EdgeCIM: A Hardware-Software Co-Design for CIM-Based Acceleration of Small
Language Models** (arXiv 2604.11512) —
[https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11512)
*Data-hygiene fix:* this item has been in `seen.json` since iteration 1
(2026-08-10) as a bare date with no annotation — never actually evaluated.
Now annotated: a custom compute-in-memory ASIC (65nm) for SLM decode,
benchmarked against an **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano** and Qualcomm SA8255P — up
to 7.3× throughput / 49.6× energy efficiency over the Orin Nano on
Llama3.2-1B. **Differentiate-from** — silicon/hardware layer, not
software/harness; not a competitor (requires custom fab, not something
buildable on off-the-shelf edge SoCs), but directly useful diligence data
if/when the postponed Jetson purchase (sub-question 5) resurfaces —
joins arXiv 2511.11624 (iter 31) as a second independent Jetson Orin Nano
energy data point.
