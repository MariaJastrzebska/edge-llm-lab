- **HARBOR: A Harness Framework for Agentic Robot Reinforcement Learning**
  (arXiv 2606.08610). **A third, unrelated paper sharing the "HARBOR" name** —
  after 2604.20938 ("HARBOR: Automated Harness Optimization," a Bayesian
  agent-config solver, tracked since iter 30) and now this one, a robotics

  manipulation/locomotion/bimanual tasks). Genuinely interesting that it sits
  in robotics, but wrong layer entirely — it automates the *RL-training*
  engineering pipeline, not SLM inference or an edge decode mechanism.
  **Ignore, but flag prominently**: "HARBOR" (one of Maria's three named
  candidates from iter 30) is now a three-way name collision across
  agent-config-optimization, agentic-coding-evaluation, and robot-RL domains —

  "HARBOR" candidate reference (if it meant something specific) should be
  disambiguated against these three if it resurfaces in conversation.
- **Harbor Adapters and Harbor-Index** (arXiv 2609.04298, Sept 2026) — a
  fourth "Harbor"-named artifact: unified evaluation infrastructure porting
  80+ agentic benchmarks and distilling an 82-task curated meta-dataset
  (Harbor-Index) for agent capability/failure-mode analysis. Pure
  evaluation-infrastructure at the agent-orchestration level, no edge/systems/
  energy angle. **Ignore.**
- **What makes a harness a harness: necessary and sufficient conditions for
  an agent harness** (arXiv 2606.10106) — a conceptual-analysis paper trying
  to pin down a reference definition of "agent harness" (the term is loosely
  used for whole products, eval scaffolds, SDKs, and orchestrators
  interchangeably), scoped to *coding* agents (Claude Code, Codex CLI,
  SWE-bench harness) via a horse-tack analogy. Wrong domain (coding agents,
  not SLMs/edge) and wrong layer (definitional, not mechanism), but a genuine
  terminology precedent — **cite**, joins 2604.08224 ("Externalization in LLM
  Agents") and 2604.18071 ("Architectural Design Decisions in AI Agent
  Harnesses") as a third citable source for how the harness-OS paper should
  define "harness" precisely in its own terms section.
- **Harness Engineering: Anatomy, Architecture, and Evolution of Coding
  Agents — A Source-Code Study of Eleven Systems** (arXiv 2609.00006) — same
  coding-agent domain as the previous item, a source-code architecture study
  of 11 coding-agent systems. **Ignore**, continues the standing pattern that
  harness-taxonomy literature stays at the coding-agent/orchestration level.
- **Pico: A Modular Framework for Hypothesis-Driven Small Language Model
  Research** (arXiv 2509.16413) — a *training*-time research sandbox for
  SLMs (targeted architecture/training-procedure ablations, ships baseline
  `pico-decoder` models). Despite the "SLM framework" phrasing that surfaces

  not an inference/deployment harness — a different meaning of "framework

- **UCCI: Calibrated Uncertainty for Cost-Optimal LLM Cascade Routing**
  (arXiv 2605.18796) — calibration-first cascade router (isotonic regression
