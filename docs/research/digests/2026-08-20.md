2. **"The Format Tax"** (arXiv 2604.03616, Apr 2026) — **cite**, joins the
   correctness-tax cluster (2605.26128, 2603.03305, 2604.14862). Tests six
   open-weight + four API models across four formats (JSON/XML/LaTeX/
   Markdown) and finds the accuracy loss is dominated by the *prompt-level*
   instruction to format (it suppresses step-by-step reasoning) rather than
   the *decoder-level* sampling constraint itself; decoupling reasoning from
   formatting (freeform-then-reformat, or extended thinking before the
   constrained pass) recovers most of the loss. This is a genuinely useful
   addition, not just a fourth confirmation: it gives the correctness-tax
   gate a candidate mechanism (prompt-level, not mask-level) and a candidate
   mitigation that is orthogonal to G1/Route A's own optimization target
   (mask-build cost) — worth a related-work paragraph distinguishing "our
   tax is decode-time/hardware, theirs is prompt-time/accuracy" once the
   correctness-tax gate is actually built into the harness.
