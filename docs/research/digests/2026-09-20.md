## Two adjacent finds outside the cluster, both worth citing

5. **"Lost in Space" / "Finding the Right Tokens for Structured Output"**
   (arXiv 2502.14969, Cornell — Hamilton & Mimno) — tests whether
   semantically-similar output-token formats (e.g. digits vs. words,
   leading-whitespace vs. not) produce systematically different zero-shot
   classification accuracy under structured output; finds up to 5–10pp
   accuracy swings from token-format choice alone, and — most relevant
   here — **format-based differences are largest for smaller models, the
   ones used for local/laptop-scale inference**. **Differentiate-from/
   cite** for the **correctness-tax cluster**, not mask-speedup — joins
   schema-key-wording (2604.14862) and "Your Prompt Is Not the Only Prompt"
   (2608.08254) as a fourth distinct "how you phrase the constraint changes
   correctness, not just the constraint itself" mechanism, and the only one
   of the four that explicitly flags the sub-1B-model regime as where the
   effect is worst — directly on point for Maria's own model class.
6. **"Keyword Matters: Unveiling the Energy Sensitivity of On-Device LLM
   Prompting"** (arXiv 2607.22568) — real smartphone power measurements
   showing prompt wording (imperative keywords, instruction structure)
   measurably changes decoding length and total energy. **Differentiate-
   from/cite** — a new mechanism family entirely (prompt phrasing as an
   energy-affecting channel, not a mask-cost or correctness channel), but
   same on-device-real-power-measurement telemetry-methodology shape as the
   lab's own harness and as prior citations (lm-Meter, Sustainable LLM
   Inference for Edge AI, "Sometimes Painful but Certainly Promising").
   Worth a related-work sentence if a future draft ever discusses prompt-
   engineering as a lever alongside G1's decode-time mechanism.
