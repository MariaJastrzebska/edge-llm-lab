## New item: Earley-Driven Dynamic Pruning / Formatron — 11th mask-speedup cluster member

**Earley-Driven Dynamic Pruning for Efficient Structured Decoding** (arXiv
2506.01151, ICML 2025 poster) —
[https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01151)

*Relevance:* Builds on the Earley-parsing approach to context-free-grammar
mask computation, adding a dynamic-pruning strategy that discards Earley
sets/states no longer referenced by later decoding steps while preserving
completeness — implemented in a tool called Formatron, up to 2× faster
constrained decoding than the prior state of the art, no accuracy loss.
*Verdict:* **differentiate-from**, joins the algorithmic mask-speedup cluster
(PSC, GRID, Attention-Meets-Reachability, Token-Space-Compression, Pre³,
XGrammar-2, MetaJuLS, Stay Within Your Bounds, Trie Automata, WGRAMMAR) as
its **11th member**. Same defining trait as all ten others: a cloud/GPU-
serving-baseline speedup number, no edge hardware, no Joules/single-stream
framing, no CPU/GPU pipelining angle. Reinforces rather than threatens
Route A/G1's gap — algorithmic mask-speedup work is a crowded, active area,
but exclusively evaluated on data-center serving stacks (vLLM/SGLang-class),
never on-device.
