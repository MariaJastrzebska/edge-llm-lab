## Harness-OS-thesis sweep — one genuine systems-layer find, eight
application-layer surveys filed to avoid re-discovery

Three queries ("small language model harness edge device systems mechanism",
"SLM agent harness on-device energy latency telemetry", "operating system
small language models edge devices runtime", "agent harness taxonomy survey
2026 new"). Eleven new-to-`seen.json` items.

**Headline find: DVFSLM** (arXiv 2609.13153, "DVFS for Small Language Model
Inference on Mobile Edge Devices," City University of Hong Kong, with a
companion MobiSys'26 workshop paper, doi:10.1145/3812836.3814753) — a
runtime DVFS (dynamic voltage/frequency scaling) governor purpose-built for
SLM decode workloads (existing DVFS governors are tuned for cloud-scale LLMs
or generic mobile workloads and don't fit SLM's characteristics), dynamically
adjusting CPU/GPU/EMC frequencies to minimize energy-per-token while meeting
a configurable token-generation deadline. **Differentiate-from/cite** — the
strongest harness-OS-thesis find in roughly ten iterations of sweeping this
angle: a genuine runtime/kernel-level governance mechanism (frequency
scaling, not grammar/mask decoding) validated on real mobile edge hardware,
energy-aware *and* deadline-aware — exactly the module shape a "Harness OS
for Small Models" would want to cite as precedent, or could eventually
incorporate as a sibling module to G1. **Not a scoop**: different mechanism
entirely from G1 (power/frequency governance vs. constrained-decoding
mask-cost tax + shadow-mask pipelining) and doesn't threaten the bigos
umbrella (one candidate module, not the whole frame) — but it is the first
paper this angle has surfaced that sits at the actual systems/runtime layer
rather than the agent-orchestration/application layer every prior
harness-taxonomy find (iters 30-35) sat at. Two adjacent DVFS-thread
citations, both **cite**: **"Dissecting the Impact of Mobile DVFS Governors
on LLM Inference Performance and Energy Efficiency"** (arXiv 2507.02135) —
the characterization study motivating DVFSLM's gap; **"Taming Asynchronous
CPU-GPU Coupling for Frequency-aware Latency Estimation on Mobile Edge"**
(arXiv 2604.15357) — a *latency-estimation* (not governance) paper under the
same frequency-coupling mechanism, adjacent but distinct role.

Eight more items, all **ignore**, continuing the standing pattern (iters
30-35) that "agent harness" literature stays at the application/
orchestration level: **"Agent Harness Engineering: A Survey"** (TMLR 2026
submission, ETCLOVG seven-layer taxonomy) — likely the same underlying
project/dataset as the already-tracked 2605.29682 (shared GitHub/HuggingFace
resources), filed under its TMLR listing to avoid re-discovery; **"From
Question Answering to Task Completion: A Survey on Agent System and Harness
Design"** (arXiv 2606.20683); **"Model or Harness? An Interaction-Centric
Taxonomy for Localizing Agent Failures"** (arXiv 2607.28802); **"Harness
Engineering as Categorical Architecture"** (arXiv 2605.12239); **"Code as
Agent Harness"** (arXiv 2605.18747); **"Harness as an Asset"** / CAAF (arXiv
2604.17025); **"HarnessX: A Composable, Adaptive, and Evolvable Agent
Harness Foundry"** (arXiv 2606.14249); **"How Much Heavy Lifting Can an
Agent Harness Do?"** (arXiv 2604.07236). None touch edge hardware, SLM-
specific systems cost, or energy/latency telemetry — the gap this loop has
